The horizontal axis is bars, not seconds. Apple's grid tracks the performance rather than laying a fixed ruler over it, so even spacing here means even musical time . Section rules come from the structure pass, which finds boundaries but does not name them, and it reports one fewer section on iOS than on macOS for identical bytes — these counts are the Mac's. Each lane's dashed rule is the loudest that family reaches anywhere in the track, so a lane sitting near zero is measured quiet rather than missing data.
